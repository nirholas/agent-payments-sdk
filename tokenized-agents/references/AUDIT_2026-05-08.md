# `tokenized-agents/references/` audit — 2026-05-08

This audit was performed as part of the v2 + dual-program rewrite of
[`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md), [`SCENARIOS.md`](../SCENARIOS.md), and
[`WALLET_INTEGRATION.md`](../WALLET_INTEGRATION.md).

## Files in `references/` as of 2026-05-08

| File | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| [`SCENARIOS.md`](SCENARIOS.md) | **stale, delete** | Five two-line scenarios that predate the dual-program reality, the v2 bonding curve, USDC quote mints, and the 3.0.x-only ix (`agent_transfer_extra_lamports`, `global_remove_currency`). The scenarios reference no specific program, so a reader cannot tell whether the described flow targets `pUmPFn9...` or `AgenTMiC...`. The current copy is also reachable as a duplicate of the (now-replaced) top-level `SCENARIOS.md` — keeping it would split sources of truth. The full replacement lives at [`../SCENARIOS.md`](../SCENARIOS.md). |
| [`WALLET_INTEGRATION.md`](WALLET_INTEGRATION.md) | **stale, delete** | A generic Solana wallet-adapter setup with no tokenized-agent specifics — no dual-program detection, no signing-rules table, no cashback v2 branch. The replacement lives at [`../WALLET_INTEGRATION.md`](../WALLET_INTEGRATION.md), which assumes the generic `WalletProvider` setup is documented elsewhere in the broader pump-fun skills (which it is, in [`../../create-coin/SKILL.md`](../../create-coin/SKILL.md)). Retaining a separate generic copy here invites readers to follow it instead of the tokenized-agent-aware version. |

## Recommended action

Delete both files. The top-level `tokenized-agents/SKILL.md` no longer
references them — it links instead to [`../SCENARIOS.md`](../SCENARIOS.md)
and [`../WALLET_INTEGRATION.md`](../WALLET_INTEGRATION.md), the rewritten
authoritative copies.

If the published-skill distribution mechanism (e.g. the
`raw.githubusercontent.com` URLs the previous SKILL.md linked to) expects
`references/SCENARIOS.md` and `references/WALLET_INTEGRATION.md` to exist
at those exact paths, the deletion should be staged together with a fix to
the consuming surface — otherwise downstream callers will 404.

## Claims in the rewrite that are explicitly marked unverified

Two items remain explicitly flagged inside the rewrite:

1. `agent_buyback_trigger` exact use of `token_agent_payment_in_currency`
   (see [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md) `agent_buyback_trigger` row for
   account #3). The IDL only declares the account list and arg type; the
   on-chain logic that consumes them is in the on-chain Rust source, which
   is not vendored under `pump-public-docs/` or `src/solana/`.
2. The current contents of `Global.whitelisted_quote_mints` (mainnet) and
   `GlobalConfig.supported_currencies_mint` (mainnet, both 1.0.7 and 3.0.x).
   These are runtime state, not IDL constants. The rewrite refers callers to
   the (yet-to-land) `docs/mainnet-verification-report.md`.
